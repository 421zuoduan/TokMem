and provenance fields solely so you can audit it. Those fields are never shown

are enforced separately when the teacher trajectory is collected. Do not reject
a task merely because another valid trajectory could reach the same final state.

offers a nonexistent or unusable tool, needs an external service, exposes
oracle/evaluator data, can pass without completing the request, relies on an
LLM judge, escapes the workspace, or cannot be reset deterministically.
